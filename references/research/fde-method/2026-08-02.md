# FDE customer-adoption and agentic-maintenance research — 2 August 2026

## Scope and boundary

Question: which public, current practices from Google, OpenAI and Anthropic should
shape an enterprise Forward Deployed Engineer playbook and an agentic handbook
maintenance system for a Google Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform repository?

This record uses public official documentation, engineering material, role
descriptions and source repositories. Public roles describe organizational intent
but are not product contracts. Google's public FDE roles are the primary source
for the field contract in this Google-oriented repository. OpenAI and Anthropic
evidence is secondary comparison material and cannot substantiate Google
capabilities.

## Google evidence

1. The [Google Cloud AI and ML Well-Architected perspective](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/perspectives/ai-ml)
   requires AI workloads to meet operational, security, reliability, cost and
   performance goals. Security guidance extends from design through ongoing
   operation and recommends continuous evaluation and monitoring.
2. Google's [five lessons for moving beyond pilots](https://cloud.google.com/transform/beyond-the-pilot-five-hard-won-lessons-from-google-clouds-ai-transformation-strategy)
   emphasize curated enterprise context, task-integrated experiences, early real
   feedback, governance and avoiding fragmented AI sprawl.
3. The [multi-tenant agentic AI reference architecture](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/multi-tenant-agentic-ai-system)
   uses centralized governance with isolated tenant projects, IAM, VPC Service
   Controls, centralized logging and Agent Runtime.
4. Current [ADK skills documentation](https://adk.dev/skills/) defines modular
   `SKILL.md`, reference, asset and script packages loaded on demand through
   `SkillToolset`.
5. Current [ADK evaluation guidance](https://adk.dev/evaluate/) supports tool
   trajectory, response, rubric, groundedness, safety and multi-turn evaluation,
   including CI execution.
6. A current [Google Cloud GenAI FDE role](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/143358812177212102-forward-deployed-engineer-genai-google-cloud)
   defines the FDE as an embedded builder who jointly ships in customer
   environments, resolves production blockers, builds evaluation and observability,
   creates reusable field patterns and product feedback, and co-builds customer
   capability.
7. The [Google Cloud FDE IV role](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/143122286080074438-forward-deployed-engineer-iv/)
   adds senior ownership of ambiguous customer outcomes, production-grade agentic
   workflows, measurable ROI, enterprise integration and adoption.
8. The [Gemini Enterprise Customer Experience FDE role](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/103848231200268998-forward-deployed-engineer/)
   emphasizes first Customer User Journeys, Terraform, enterprise knowledge and
   infrastructure integration, live debugging, incident response and adoption.
9. A [Gemini Enterprise A2UI implementation guide](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/developers-practitioners/guide-to-gemini-enterprise-and-a2ui-integration),
   co-authored by a Google Cloud FDE, provides a runnable ADK/A2A pattern and shows
   field engineering material being converted into public implementation guidance.
10. A recent [Google DeepMind FDE role](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/76720917111022278-forward-deployed-engineer/)
    describes partner embedding, production GenAI architecture, critical-launch
    escalation, joint benchmarking, partner-specific evaluations, reference
    implementations and a feedback loop to model teams. The posting is volatile
    evidence and can disappear when the role closes.

Qualified Google implementation remains ADK Python `v2.6.1`, commit
`740582e9f283cd23ff5cec1389400b422513f765`. The current upstream branch observed
during research was `f4e7233469e3595336dfb0d84c281b2f6245ce4c`; it is not promoted
by this record. Official sample and starter-pack pins remain in
`references/versions.json`.

## Anthropic evidence

1. Anthropic's [Applied AI Engineer role](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5343697008),
   linked through its official careers system, emphasizes deep technical
   partnership, evaluation, agent architecture, context engineering, cost,
   pair-programming, prototypes, code contributions, reusable tooling, technical
   education and customer workshops.
2. [Building effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents)
   recommends starting with simple prompts/workflows, measuring them, and adding
   agentic complexity only where simpler systems fall short.
3. [Demystifying evals for AI agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents)
   treats evaluation as a lifecycle system and combines graders and trajectory
   evidence rather than relying on a single final-answer score.

## Google-first synthesized field contract

Google's public evidence directly supports these FDE responsibilities; the
OpenAI and Anthropic material corroborates some of them but does not define the
Google role:

1. Own the path from ambiguous workflow discovery to stable production adoption.
2. Stay hands-on in architecture, code, integration, evaluation and deployment.
3. Measure business workflow impact, user adoption and operational behavior.
4. Start simple and increase autonomy only through evidence.
5. Treat security, data, reliability, cost and change as design inputs.
6. Pair with customer teams and transfer demonstrated capability.
7. Convert field learning into reusable tools, skills, evaluations and platform
   improvements.
8. Feed real failures and constraints back to product and research.

The repository implements this contract through the customer adoption playbook,
the dedicated Google FDE operating model, machine-checkable engagement kit, six
reusable maintenance skills, a bounded ADK author/reviewer team, content-addressed
proposal policy and draft-only GitHub workflow.

## Unknowns and review triggers

- Public role descriptions can change and must be treated as dated evidence.
- No public Google source represents Google's complete internal FDE method, staffing
  investment, customer entitlement or contractual delivery process.
- Cross-vendor patterns require customer and Google-specific review before use.
- Moving repository heads are observations, not qualified baselines.
- Re-review on material role changes, ADK skill/evaluation API change, customer
  pilot evidence, or maintainer workflow incident.
