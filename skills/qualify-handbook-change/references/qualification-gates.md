# Qualification gates

| Gate | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| research | official evidence is current, pinned, scoped, and non-contradictory |
| architecture | decision traces to customer constraint and viable alternatives |
| implementation | code/IaC compiles or validates; contracts and negative paths are tested |
| security | identity, data, network, tools, actions, supply chain, tenants and audit fail closed |
| operations | SLO, telemetry, incident, recovery, rollback, support and cost are executable |
| delivery | an FDE can run discovery-to-handover without undocumented knowledge |
| independence | reviewer differs from author and unresolved material findings block publication |

Cloud, load, recovery, penetration, industry, adoption, and customer acceptance evidence must identify the real environment and executor. Example records and generated evidence cannot be marked as passed customer qualification.
