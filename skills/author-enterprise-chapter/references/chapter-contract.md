# Enterprise chapter contract

Every substantive capability must answer:

1. What customer workflow and measurable outcome does it serve?
2. When should the customer not use an agent or this capability?
3. What is officially supported, and at what maturity and location?
4. What are the logical and physical architectures and trust boundaries?
5. What exact implementation produces a thin vertical slice?
6. How are identity, data, tools, actions, network, secrets, supply chain, and tenants controlled?
7. How are quality, safety, latency, reliability, cost, and business value evaluated?
8. What fails, how is it detected, what is the manual fallback, and how is it recovered or rolled back?
9. What must the customer own before production and after handover?
10. Which evidence would change the decision?

Use the repository chapter template for final section names. Prefer compact decision tables, executable examples, negative tests, and customer acceptance evidence over product-feature narration.
