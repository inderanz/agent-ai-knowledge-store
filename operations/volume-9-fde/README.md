# Volume 9 FDE delivery operations

## Weekly engagement control

Review outcome signal, completed evidence, blockers/RAID, access and environment,
security/privacy/domain decisions, spend, customer capability and next irreversible
decision. Publish a concise truth-based status: achieved, observed, unknown,
blocked, decision needed, owner and date.

## Escalate immediately

- requested access/action exceeds charter or customer authority is unclear;
- real sensitive data appears in an unapproved environment or artifact;
- prohibited/material autonomy is introduced without customer review;
- current product maturity/location/terms invalidate the design;
- an unsafe or unknown business write may have occurred;
- launch pressure asks reviewers to relabel an open blocker; or
- production depends on FDE personal identity, laptop, secret or undocumented knowledge.

## Exit control

Remove/expire FDE access; transfer repositories, IaC, pipelines, evidence and
support paths into customer control; verify backups/retention; run competency and
incident exercises; record known limitations and residual risks; set 30/60/90-day
reviews; and obtain named customer acceptance. Engagement completion is not
production success unless outcome and operations continue after the FDE exits.
