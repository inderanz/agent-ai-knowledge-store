# Volume 8 industry operations

## Containment priorities

Protect people and material rights; stop prohibited or unsafe actions; preserve
the authoritative domain record; prevent cross-subject disclosure; route to the
qualified manual/offline process; preserve minimal approved evidence; notify the
customer's domain, safety, legal/compliance, privacy/security and communications
owners according to their plan.

## Industry signals

- wrong subject/account/patient/claim/case/asset/configuration applicability;
- stale or superseded authoritative source;
- prohibited material decision or mutated approval;
- unknown or duplicate ledger/order/claim/work-order effect;
- sensitive data in prompts, metadata, telemetry, build or evidence;
- domain quality, language, subgroup or accessibility regression;
- manual/offline queue and qualified reviewer capacity; and
- service/contract/maturity/location or customer-overlay drift.

## Recovery

Do not let the model adjudicate business truth. Reconcile with the authoritative
ledger/record, restore controlled sources/configuration/state/evidence, validate
identity and domain applicability, replay only idempotently, have the qualified
owner review affected cases, communicate through the customer's approved process,
and requalify the industry overlay before resuming material actions.
