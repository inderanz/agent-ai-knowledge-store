# Gemini Enterprise app operations pack

Own the app, data stores/connectors, ACL synchronization, agents/actions, model
and feature settings as one user-facing service. Monitor ingestion/index freshness,
connector auth/errors/quota, ACL-denial tests, retrieval/answer quality, citation
coverage, latency/errors, agent/action success and deny, Gateway failures, active
users, feedback, cost and data-location/CMEK posture. Telemetry must be privacy
reviewed and access controlled.

Severity-one triggers include unauthorized result/action, cross-user data leak,
systemic unsafe answer/action, broad IAM exposure and unrecoverable connector
corruption. Contain by disabling the affected agent/action/data store or app
access, revoke credentials, preserve audit/evaluation evidence and reconcile
actions. Maintain source-of-truth reindex, connector reauthorization, configuration
reconstruction, data export/retention and app deletion/exit runbooks. Recovery is
proven only by ACL, relevance, citation, safety and action tests—not by a green UI.
